Victoria Peak (The Peak)

The last and definitely not the least attraction to add to your itinerary is Victoria Peak (also known as The Peak). If you love nature, city lights, and breathtaking views, then this place is for you. It is known for its tram, sky terrace, parks, and commercial centers. In order to get to the top, you must ride the tram and when you get there, you'll be able to explore the peak tower which encompasses numerous shops, dining options, and the famous open-air 360-degree observation deck where you'll be able to see the Hong Kong Skyline. It offers panoramic views of Victoria Harbour, Central, Kowloon, and surrounding islands. Based on my experience, It's a 100% worth it to visit this place. The tram experience was fun and very steep, it took us 15+ minutes to get to the top. When we finally got to the tower, it was quite similar to a mall (because it's also filled with restaurants and shops) but it's better because it's on a mountain and the view is unexplainable... And not to mention that I was able to see some wax figures of different celebrities along the way. I also saw the famous Bubba Gump Shrimp Co. Furthermore, When we got to the Sky Terrace we finally got to see the exquisite view of the skyline, the city lights were very bright, it's cold and it's also a little cloudy. It was so mesmerizing. For me, being up there was lowkey terrifying for me (because I'm not very good with heights) however it also brought peace and tranquility because of the environment and the amazing view. This place is so "Peak" and it's very instagrammable. Most importantly, you can also bring your family and friends so that you'll be able to spend your time with them on a beautiful place.
