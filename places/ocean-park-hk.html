Ocean Park Hong Kong (香港海洋公園)

Ocean Park Hong Kong is not as simple as you think it is. It is not only limited to marine life because it's also an amusement park. I recommend that you add this on your itinerary as it offers a wide-range of activities and attractions. If you visit this place, expect to see or encounter different kinds of sea creatures and their massive tanks. For instance, The Grand Aquarium inside Ocean Park HK is one of the world's top aquariums as it houses 5000 fishes across 400 species. There are also different exhibits and programs that you will see. Personally, I was able to see penguins, sea lions, walruses, sharks, and we even saw a panda. As a shark enthusiast, one of my favorite exhibits is the Shark Mystique as it showcases different species of sharks. As for the other activities, there are also numerous rides that you can enjoy with your friends and family. Rides like rollercoasters, a ferris wheel, cable car, and so much more. In terms of food and restaurants, there are a lot of quick service or fast-food restaurants within the place. I was able to try their fried chicken, french fries, and onion rings... and believe me they were genuinely good so make sure to checkout some QSRs or food stores. Ocean Park Hong Kong will always have a special place in my heart as it gave me one of the best experiences in Hong Kong seeing people make memories there (while making my own) makes my heart very happy as I see their genuine joy through their bright smiles.
