Hong Kong Disneyland (香港迪士尼樂園)

I fully recommend Hong Kong Disneyland as it is regarded as the "Happiest Place on Earth" by people---including me. And It's true, the moment you step inside disneyland, you'll feel nothing but genuine happiness because of the wonderful places, restaurants, rides, activities, and disney characters around you. In this theme park, everybody is welcome as it offers family-friendly activities. Upon visiting this attaction, it is certain that you will see some locations or activities that are themed with a disney movie or a series that you know. Based-on my experience, I was able to visit Toy Story Land, an area within disneyland that is specifically toy story themed. Another memorable activity that I experienced, is the (MARVEL) Iron Man Star Expo Experience where I got to meet Iron Man himself. Lastly, we also rode a Star Wars themed roller coaster which is so dark and you will not be able to see the tracks. The experience was amazing because of the space and rockets. Both Disney and Non-Disney Fans are going to enjoy this place. This is where they can do a lot of fun activities with their loved ones. And it's a place for healing your inner child.
